## How the numbers were made

- An object counts as **realistic** when its `raw/<id>.meta.json` says the source is Sketchfab. Every Sketchfab model in the project is CC-BY or CC0 and was chosen for the realistic look.
- **Drawn** means the source is Poly Pizza, Kenney or Poly Haven low-poly.
- **In use** means a lesson's `objects[].model` or a land's `model` field names it. Files nothing names are listed in section 8.
- The Sketchfab column comes from `GET /v3/search` with `downloadable=true`, `max_face_count=100000`, once for `license=by` and once for `license=cc0`, merged. Hit counts are capped at 24 per licence, so 48 means "plenty".
